# Canon — The Loom Is Celestial, Not Textile (June 2026)

We keep the words **Thread · Weave · Loom · Working** but redefine their imagery from
cloth to **light**. The metaphor is now fully consistent with the rest of Sideros
(Comet-Magi, the Fall into the twelve signs, the Aether, Resonance).

## The redefinition
- **Aether** — the field of raw starlight and possibility (the charged dark between stars).
- **Thread** — a *line of starlight*: the line drawn between stars, the way a star-chart
  joins points into a figure. (Not a fiber of cloth.)
- **To Weave** — to *draw* Aetheric Threads into a figure that holds for a moment.
  (Not to interlace cloth — to trace lines of light.)
- **Working** — the figure traced; a small constellation called into being for an instant.
- **The Loom** — the cosmic engine that *draws the constellations across the sky*. When the
  gods fell, the Loom drew them into the twelve great signs and never stopped drawing.
  (Not a wooden frame.)

## The payoff (unchanged, now celestial)
Magic and Fate are the **same drawn starlight at two scales**: the Loom draws the great
figures (the signs, and the lines of fate between souls — Fated Bonds are *starlines*); a
Weaver draws small figures (Workings) from the same light. "The Loom weaves all; your thread
is your own" now reads as: the engine draws every line, but your own starline is yours.

## Writing rule
Keep the four words. **Avoid textile imagery** — no cloth, fabric, tapestry, spun, spindle,
fiber, yarn, weft/warp, or "strand" used as fiber. Reach instead for: starlight, lines drawn
between stars, tracing a figure, the dark between stars, light pulled from the Aether.
